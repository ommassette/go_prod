Privacy Policy

Effective date: 5 October 2026

01 — WHAT WE COLLECT

Information we collect

We only collect what you choose to give us — through the forms on this site. Nothing more, nothing hidden. Specifically:

  • Contact requests — when you submit the form on our contact page, we receive your name, email address, phone number (if provided), chosen topic and message.
  • Feedback submissions — when you share feedback on the home page, we receive your name, email and the feedback itself.
  • Questions — when you ask a question through the FAQ page, we receive your name, email, chosen subject and message.
We don't track you. No analytics cookies, no third-party trackers, no advertising IDs, no fingerprinting.
02 — HOW WE USE IT

How we use your information

Whatever you send us is used for one purpose only: to get back to you and to run this service well. In practice, that means:

  • Replying to your inquiries, questions and feedback.
  • Providing details about our pricing plans, add-ons and services.
  • Improving the reliability, speed and functionality of the site.
  • Keeping the platform secure and diagnosing technical issues.
03 — STORAGE & SECURITY

Data retention & security

Form submissions are stored securely on servers we control. We use industry-standard security practices to protect against unauthorised access, alteration or loss of your information.

We keep submissions only as long as they're needed to respond to you and maintain our records — after which they can be deleted on request.

04 — SHARING

Third-party sharing

We don't sell, trade, rent or otherwise hand your personal information to third parties. Period.

Information you send us is processed strictly for internal communication and customer support — nothing else.

05 — YOUR RIGHTS

Your rights

You can ask us at any time what information we hold about you, request a copy, or ask us to delete it. Just get in touch through the contact page and we'll take care of it.

06 — GET IN TOUCH

Questions?

If anything here is unclear, or you'd like to exercise any of the rights above, reach out through our contact page — a real person will respond.

Cookie Policy

Effective date: 5 October 2026

01 — OVERVIEW

What are cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help us keep forms secure, maintain your session across pages, and serve content smoothly.

02 — WHAT WE USE

Cookies we use

We keep things minimal. The only cookies we set are strictly necessary for the site to function safely and correctly:

  • CSRF token cookie (csrftoken) — required for Cross-Site Request Forgery protection on every form submission across the site, including our feedback, question and contact forms.
  • Session cookie — used to carry flash messages such as submission confirmations across page redirects.
We don't use advertising or analytics cookies. No third-party trackers, no behavioural profiling, no cross-site advertising IDs.
03 — PERFORMANCE

Performance & caching

To keep pages like our pricing plans and add-ons fast, we use server-side caching. This happens entirely on our own servers and does not track individual browsing behaviour across external sites.

04 — YOUR CHOICE

Managing cookies

You can disable or delete cookies at any time through your browser settings. Just note that turning off essential cookies — particularly the CSRF token — will prevent contact forms and other submissions from working correctly.

05 — GET IN TOUCH

Questions?

If you'd like more detail on how we handle cookies, or anything else about your data, reach out through our contact page — a real person will answer.
